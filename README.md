<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/AI_Related-7B2FBE?style=for-the-badge&logo=probot&logoColor=white" style="height: 35px;" />

*   **[Agentic-Workflow Plugin](https://github.com/gidde032/AgenticWorkflow)** - My custom plugin, made for engineers looking to create a consistent structure around their agents. Currently comprising 12 skills, it covers all steps of the typical development process, from turning a rough idea into an initial spec to parallel multi-subagent code reviews before shipping, backed by GitHub's native PR, Issues, and Actions infrastructure. Designed to promote understanding of agentic development and assist users in maximizing their agentic tooling, the plugin naturally produces documentation as you work, noting your typical usage, recording your preferred working style, and gathering references to help tailor the plugin's skills to your own purposes each session. Skills, evals, and refinements are regularly added; please feel free to open an Issue with any feedback or suggestions!
*   **Ajax** - A locally-hosted one-stop hub for routing agents through your digital ecosystem. Combines project management, calendar scheduling, and agent dispatch, built on a local SQLite database with TypeScript and Python. Features include: scanning a provided root to automatically detect projects, uploading your existing Roadmap files to scaffold tasks instantly, organizing backlog items and issues using customizable Kanban boards, writing and attaching notes to pass along with tasks, and dispatching agents from the provider of your choice using bounded-context packets built from Ajax's project data.

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/My_Tools-1E6FD9?style=for-the-badge&logo=dependabot&logoColor=white" style="height: 35px;" />

*   **[Flytie](https://pypi.org/project/flytie/)** - A Python-powered tool designed to help fly fishermen manage their fly patterns and tying materials. Read about my workflow and architecture design in my [Medium article series on Agentic Development](https://medium.com/@finn.gidden/building-flytie-setting-up-the-proper-structure-for-agentic-development-7ac1f58ea8ff).
*   **[RicePoster](https://github.com/gidde032/RicePoster)** - An automated Instagram and TikTok posting tool with a Python backend and a simple, single-page HTML UI, powered by FastAPI and Playwright. Post to up to 12 accounts at once, generate input-free captions with the Anthropic API using automated post snapshots, schedule batches of posts for future dates, review your post history, and clear your stale media directory with one press of a button.
*   **[ITGKB](https://github.com/gidde032/itgkb)** - A space-themed TypeScript visualizer for a Markdown IT Knowledge Base. Contains templates and instructions for automated and manual content ingestion for new article creation. Articles can be searched by text and are clustered by category and topic; semantic search and grouping are planned for future versions.

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Bits_and_Bobs-1B4332?style=for-the-badge&logo=itchdotio&logoColor=white" style="height: 35px;" />

*   **[Driftwood](https://github.com/gidde032/driftwood)** - A [single-file, HTML, 8-bit fishing game](https://gidde032.github.io/driftwood/) inspired by Stardew Valley's angling minigame. Fill out your Fishopedia with over 60 unique species across 4 seasons and 7 locations, load up your tacklebox with dozens of lures, build your aquarium, unlock achievements, and find the Ferryman to guide you towards the source of the mysterious humming. Daily quests, lake records, rival anglers, and random competition events provide variety and local competition; universal 'Catch of the Day' tasks are shared across all copies of Driftwood, designed so that you can export your catch cards and gloat to your fishing buddies. If you can cement yourself as a Driftwood master angler, start a New Game+ to restart with just a twig and a bobber and do it all over again.
*   **[Adventures of Juggman](https://github.com/gidde032/roguelikeAdventure)** - A C-based roguelike dungeon crawler, procedurally generated so that no two runs are the same. Take control of Juggman as he collects potions, opens chests, and battles goblins, spiders, and trolls. Clear the fog as you peer around corners, using Bresenham's algorithm to determine the player's FOV, and look for the ladder to ascend to the next floor.
